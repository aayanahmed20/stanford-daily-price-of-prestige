# Glossary

Terms as used in *The Price of Prestige*. Definitions follow the College
Scorecard's technical documentation unless noted.

## Cost

- **Sticker price / cost of attendance (`COSTT4_A`)** — published tuition,
  fees, room and board, books and supplies, and other expenses for full-time,
  first-time undergraduate students. No financial aid is subtracted.

- **Net price (`NPT4_PRIV`)** — cost of attendance minus the average grant and
  scholarship aid a student actually received. This is the price students
  really pay.

- **Net price by income bracket (`NPT41_PRIV` … `NPT45_PRIV`)** — net price
  for dependent students grouped by family income: $0-30k, $30-48k, $48-75k,
  $75-110k, and $110k+.

- **Negative net price** — aid exceeds the full cost of attendance, so the
  average family in that bracket pays nothing out of pocket (and often
  receives a refund toward other expenses).

## Debt

- **Median cumulative debt (`GRAD_DEBT_MDN`)** — median total debt (all
  student loans, all holders) of bachelor's graduates.

- **Low/high-income borrower debt (`LO_INC_DEBT_MDN`, `HI_INC_DEBT_MDN`)** —
  median debt of graduates whose families are in the lowest / highest income
  third.

## Completion and enrollment

- **Six-year completion rate (`C150_4`)** — share of the entering cohort that
  completes a credential within 150% of the normal time (six years for a
  four-year degree).

- **Pell share (`PCTPELL`)** — share of degree-seeking undergraduates who
  receive a federal Pell Grant, the standard proxy for low- to
  middle-income enrollment.

- **First-generation share (`PAR_ED_PCT_1STGEN`)** — share of degree-seeking
  undergraduates whose parents have no postsecondary education.

- **Admission rate (`ADM_RATE`)** — share of applicants admitted.

## Post-college outcomes

- **Ten-year earnings (`MD_EARN_WNE_P10`)** — median earnings of the cohort
  that first enrolled roughly ten years earlier, including both graduates and
  non-graduates. Percentiles (`PCT25`/`PCT75`/`PCT90`) describe the earnings
  distribution.

- **Repayment rate (`RPY_3YR_RT`)** — share of student-loan borrowers who are
  repaying (not in default, deferment, or forbearance) three years into
  repayment.

## Economics

- **Real dollars** — amounts adjusted for inflation and expressed in a single
  reference year (2025 here), using the CPI-U. Real values are comparable
  across time; nominal values are not.

- **Residual** — the difference between an observed value and a model's
  prediction. Stanford's earnings *residual* is how much its graduates out-
  or under-earn a school with the same price, selectivity, and student-income
  profile.
